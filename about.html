About IGM

A trusted facility and janitorial partner.

Inovative General Maintenance serves commercial, industrial, and institutional clients in the San Diego area — reliability, professionalism, and attention to detail.

We deliver facility and janitorial work tailored to how a building actually runs — offices that need to look occupied, industrial spaces that cannot wait on a showpiece crew, and institutional sites that expect the same standard every night.

The team is built around reliability, professionalism, and attention to detail. That means clear communication, crews who know the assignment, and interiors that stay safe, clean, and presentable so people can do their jobs.

We use practical technology to keep cleaning processes consistent and to keep the conversation with the client simple. The aim is a long working relationship, not a one-time sweep.
